No big local GPU? Render the talking avatar on Colab's free GPU instead. Download your audio, open the notebook (one click), and run it.

  1. Download your generated voice (the MP3 to be spoken) ⬇ Audio
  2. Open the notebook in Colab — loads straight from GitHub, no upload Open Colab ↗
  3. In Colab: set Runtime → GPU, run the cells, upload your audio + a photo/video when asked
  4. Pick Wav2Lip or SadTalker → the last cell previews and downloads your MP4

Notebook didn't open? Download it, then use File → Upload notebook in Colab. (Repo must be public for the one-click link.)
